Alembic Notes:
alembic init	Initializes Alembic in your project, creating necessary folders and configuration files (run once at project setup).
alembic revision --autogenerate -m "Msg"	Generates a new revision file based on detected schema changes (autogenerate), adding your custom message.
alembic show <revision_name>	Displays the contents of a specific revision file.
alembic upgrade head	Applies changes from all revisions up to the latest (head) to your database.
alembic downgrade <revision_name>	Reverts your database schema to the state defined in the specified revision file.
alembic history	Lists all available Alembic revisions in your project.
